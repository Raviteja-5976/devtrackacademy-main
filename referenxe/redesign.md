## Goal

Redesign the existing homepage while **keeping the exact same design language, visual identity, colors, typography, animation style, interactions, and overall premium feel**.

The website should continue to look like a modern Neo-Brutalist product website with GSAP storytelling, Framer Motion micro interactions, Lenis smooth scrolling, floating objects, thick borders, oversized typography and playful layouts.

**Only the content structure changes.**

The homepage should no longer promote workshops directly.

Instead, it introduces **DevTrackAcademy** as a learning ecosystem that consists of two independent platforms.

```
DevTrackAcademy

↓

Workshop Platform

↓

Learning Platform
```

Each should have its own dedicated landing page.

## Overall Experience

The homepage should no longer feel like a course landing page. Instead, it should feel like the central hub of the DevTrackAcademy ecosystem—a premium, modern gateway that communicates a clear philosophy: **developers are made by building, practicing, and solving real problems, not by memorizing theory.** Visitors should immediately understand that they can choose between **live practical workshops** and an **interactive learning platform**, with smooth GSAP storytelling, playful Neo-Brutalist visuals, and polished interactions that make the experience feel alive.
